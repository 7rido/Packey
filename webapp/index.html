🔒 Privacy

Stored: your chat ID, the tracking numbers you add, the names on your packages (the ones you set, or the recipient's name read off a label photo), their status history, plus your Pro payment records (amount, token, signature, and the address reserved for you) — encrypted at rest (AES + HMAC) on the bot owner's machine only. Nothing else.

Shared with: official USPS APIs for USPS numbers; 17TRACK only for international and CN/HK numbers. Pro payments are public on the Solana blockchain: the amount and the receiving address are part of that record, while the wallet memo never carries your chat ID. The personal address the sheet shows you is stable, so payments made to it are linked to one another on-chain — the QR / payment-link route is the unlinked one. No ads, no analytics, no resale.

In the chat: cards and /list delete themselves a moment after they're sent, and status pings say only that something changed — tracking numbers don't pile up in chat history.

Updates: automatic and paced per package; delivered ones are never checked again. No refresh buttons — nobody can trigger extra lookups.

Deleted by you: Remove (one package), /purge (delivered), /clear (everything, instantly). Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Deleted for you: delivered packages are forgotten automatically — the bot keeps no tracking number it no longer needs.
